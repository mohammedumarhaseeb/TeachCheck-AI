🎯 TeachCheck AI

"Don't just watch the lesson. Verify what it teaches."

TeachCheck AI is an automated video consistency checker for educational programming lectures. It cross-references what the instructor says against what code appears on screen and what the code actually executes to catch teaching bugs before students get confused.
